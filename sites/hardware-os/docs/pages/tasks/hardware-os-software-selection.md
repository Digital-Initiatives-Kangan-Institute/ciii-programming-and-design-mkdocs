# Hardware, OS, and Software Selection

---

## Scenario

A friend of yours runs a small business from home selling handmade crafts online. They need a new computer because their old laptop is slow and keeps running out of storage space. They have asked for your help choosing what to buy.

Your friend is not technical — they do not know what a CPU is, how much RAM they need, or which operating system to pick. They trust you to research and recommend the right hardware, operating system, and software for them.

### What Your Friend Needs

The computer will be used for:

- Browsing the web and managing their online shop
- Emailing customers and suppliers
- Storing and editing product photos
- Creating invoices, price lists, and social media posts
- Video calls with clients

They have a budget of around $1500 AUD for the computer and a monitor. They do not need a laptop — a desktop is fine.

---

## Exercise 1: Select Hardware

!!! abstract "Instructions"
    Research and recommend the hardware components for your friend's new computer. For each component listed below, write down what you recommend and a short explanation of why — in plain language your friend would understand.

    Components to research:

    - CPU
    - RAM
    - Storage (type and size)
    - Monitor
    - Keyboard and mouse
    - Any other accessories you think they need

    Think about what is appropriate for their needs, not the most expensive option. Your friend does not do gaming, video editing, or 3D modelling.

??? hint "Hint - What to look for"
    A home business computer does not need high-end parts. Focus on enough RAM for multitasking, fast storage (SSD) for quick startup, and a monitor large enough to work comfortably. Look at complete desktop packages from retailers like JB Hi-Fi, Officeworks, or online stores to get an idea of pricing. Your total should stay within the $1500 budget.

---

## Exercise 2: Select an Operating System

!!! abstract "Instructions"
    Using the hardware you chose in Exercise 1, research and recommend an operating system for your friend's computer. Write a short recommendation that covers:

    - Two operating systems you considered
    - Which one you recommend and why
    - Whether it costs money or is free
    - Whether it will run the software they need (web browser, email, photo editing, office apps)

    Remember your friend is not technical — your recommendation should be easy to understand.

??? hint "Hint - Where to start"
    Most home and small business computers run Windows, but there are other options. Think about what your friend needs to do and which operating system makes those tasks easiest. Consider what happens if something goes wrong — can your friend get help from a local computer shop? Is the operating system easy to learn with online tutorials?

---

## Exercise 3: Select Application Software

!!! abstract "Instructions"
    Your friend needs more than just a web browser. For each category below, research and recommend one software product. Write a brief description of what it does and whether it is free or paid.

    Categories to research:

    - A web browser
    - An email application (or web-based email service)
    - Software for creating invoices and price lists
    - Software for editing product photos
    - Backup software or cloud storage
    - Security/antivirus software

    Organise your recommendations in a table with columns for the software name, what it does, and the cost.

??? hint "Hint - Software categories to explore"
    For basic photo editing, your friend does not need Photoshop — look for simpler tools. For office work like invoices, consider Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, or free options like LibreOffice. For backup, think about whether an external hard drive, cloud storage, or both is best. For security, Windows includes built-in protection — is that enough? Look up what reviewers recommend for small business owners.
